Judge the final reply (a preliminary screen of a U.S. import situation).

PASS only if every condition below holds.
- All seven sections of the screen are present in this order: What may be wrong, What money may be at stake, What date or deadline MAY matter, What fact is missing, Measures that may apply (signal only), Why a professional might look, Next step. Short labels such as Money, Dates and Missing are accepted for sections 2 to 4.
- Every date line carries a basis and a verify step, and no POTENTIAL line is presented as certain.
- The only POTENTIAL lines are the kinds and dates listed here; a POTENTIAL line of any other kind fails (UNKNOWN lines of any kind are fine): LIQUIDATION POTENTIAL 2027-05-20.
- The basis of the LIQUIDATION line cites 1504.
- It describes the CF-28 reply period as the one printed on the form and computes no due date.
- It may use these terms only while explaining that they do not apply: 30 days.

FAIL if the reply does any of these:
- tells the user what or when to file or reply, in these words or a clear equivalent: "you have 30 days"; "reply by 2026-10-02" (exempt: the mandated sentence that a date may already be closed and whether anything can still be done is for the attorney to assess, the Next-step sentences, and the same words inside a negation or attributed to the form or a guide)

Judge substance, not formatting. A missing or unmet condition above is FAIL.
