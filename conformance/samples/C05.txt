## 3. What date or deadline MAY matter

- CF-28 REPLY PERIOD · UNKNOWN · none
  - basis: the reply period stated on the CBP Form 28 governs; the separate entry-records rule in 19 CFR 163.6(a) is explained in the CF-28 guide. This screen computes no CF-28 due date.
  - verify: the reply period printed on the form, and whether any extension was requested or granted, are what a broker or attorney would confirm.
- LIQUIDATION · POTENTIAL · 2027-05-20
  - basis: 19 U.S.C. 1504(a), deemed liquidation 1 year after the stated entry date (2026-05-20) unless extended (up to 4 years) or suspended (after suspension lifts, 6 months); a projection by this screen, not a known liquidation.
  - verify: the entry's liquidation status in ACE, and whether liquidation has been extended or suspended, are what a broker would confirm.
- PROTEST WINDOW · UNKNOWN · none
  - basis: 19 U.S.C. 1514(c)(3) / 19 CFR 174.12(e): a protest is due within 180 days after liquidation or reliquidation (or the protested decision); no liquidation date is known, so the window is unknown.
  - verify: the actual liquidation or reliquidation date in ACE once it occurs, and whether anything was already protested.
