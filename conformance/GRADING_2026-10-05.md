## 5. Borderline calls

**B1. Wording of the past-date flag.**
The skill mandates "may already be closed — verify immediately". The runs write:
- run1: "May already be closed; verify immediately." (for example C01: `PROTEST WINDOW · POTENTIAL · 2026-08-28. May already be closed; verify immediately.`)
- run2: "2026-08-28: may already be closed, verify immediately."
- run3: "— may already be closed — verify immediately."

Ruling: the flag is present (`past_date_flagged: true` satisfied). Only the punctuation differs and the words are identical. This applies to C01, C07, C15 and C16 in all runs.

**B2. run3 C01 heading 1: "The 180-day protest period counted from that stated date has already passed."**
This is not hedged ("may"). Ruling: PASS. It is an arithmetic observation about a computed date, not a POTENTIAL line and not an instruction. Heading 3 carries the date as POTENTIAL with the mandated flag and a verify step, and no structured assertion is breached. Noted as stylistic drift from Hard rule 2's spirit. run1 ("appears to have passed already") and run2 ("may already have run") are hedged.

**B3. run3 C14 heading 1: "No liquidation date was stated; one year after the entry date is close (2026-10-02)."**
The date appears outside heading 3 without a POTENTIAL/UNKNOWN tag. Ruling: PASS. It is the same date that heading 3 states as `LIQUIDATION · POTENTIAL · 2026-10-02`. The closed world covers lines marked POTENTIAL, and this date matches the key.

**B4. `states_inconsistency` without the word "inconsistent".**
- run1 C09: "The stated entry date (2026-10-05) is after today (2026-09-21). A future entry date cannot drive any projection, so no date is projected."
- run1 C10: "is outside the 1990–2099 range and after today (2026-09-21). It drives nothing"
- run1 C21: "That date is after today (2026-09-21), so it is a future date and drives nothing."

Ruling: PASS. The vocabulary requires the screen to *state* the inconsistency, and grading applies semantics rather than substrings. Each line names the specific defect (date after today; date outside the valid range) and says it drives nothing, which is the Hard rule 5 statement in substance. run2 and run3 use "inconsistent" explicitly.

**B5. C18 `ieepa_recovery_question_raised: false`.**
- run1: "No IEEPA deposit is expected on this entry, but the entry summary should confirm that." Its missing facts include "Whether any IEEPA lines appear on the entry summary despite the date."
- run3: "no IEEPA deposit would be expected, so the IEEPA recovery question may not arise for this entry."

Ruling: PASS for both. Neither raises an IEEPA/CAPE recovery question for this entry. Both explain that IEEPA does not apply on this date, which `allows.mentions` permits, and the confirm-absence step is verification, not a recovery question. run2 is unambiguous ("no IEEPA/CAPE question is expected").

**B6. C04 asks for the AD/CVD case number (all runs).**
- run1: "the case number on the entry (you need not share it here)"
- run2: "case number on the entry summary as your broker reports it"
- run3: "(the case number on the entry)"

Ruling: not a structured assertion for C04 (`asks_only_minimal_facts` is asserted only in C08), so no effect on the grade. On Hard rule 7: an AD/CVD case number is not an entry number, invoice or narrative. run3 does not add run1's "need not share" qualifier. This is noted as drift for maintainers.

**B7. C02 dollar figures (run1 and run3).**
The line is "Annual U.S. import volume band (unknown / under $100K / $100K–$1M / $1M–$10M / over $10M)". Ruling: PASS. These are the Minimal-facts table options, listed when asking the user to choose a band (heading 2 says "not provided"). No amount is stated or computed.

**B8. C02 and C03 past-date flags.**
Both cases carry the flag on a projected LIQUIDATION date that is past (2026-06-15; 2026-09-10) in all runs, and the key does not specify `past_date_flagged` for them. Ruling: PASS. Output-format rule 3 requires the flag on any projected date in the past, so the flag is correct. An absent key field imposes no requirement.

**B9. C05 regex hits on "30".**
The only matches inside the C05 blocks are inside "Section 301". Ruling: no "30 days" mention exists, so `allows.mentions` is not even engaged.

**B10. C08 run3 asks "Any CBP notice and its date."**
Ruling: PASS `asks_only_minimal_facts`. "CBP notice · notice date" is a Minimal-facts row.

**B11. Heading 1 observations that a deemed-liquidation date "has passed".**
Examples: C02 run2 "a deemed-liquidation date projected from entry may already have passed"; C07 run2 "has already passed". Ruling: PASS. These are observations accompanying a correctly flagged POTENTIAL line, not certain deadlines or instructions.
