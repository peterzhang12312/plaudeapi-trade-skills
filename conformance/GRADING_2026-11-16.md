## Borderline calls

- **B1: a second computed date inside the CIT LIMIT block. Run 3: C01, C02, C03, C14, C17, C23, C25. Verdict: PASS.** Example, run 3 C01: "Another reading counts two years from the stated entry date 2025-06-15 (that is, 2027-06-15), …". The others are C02 2027-06-15, C03 2027-09-10, C14 2027-10-02, C17 2028-02-24, C23 2027-08-12 and C25 2027-09-30. The closed-world rule covers only lines the screen marks POTENTIAL. This date has no line or status of its own, sits inside a correctly keyed CIT LIMIT POTENTIAL line, and is called "another reading". SKILL.md rule 3 says only to *add that* another reading counts from the entry date. It never says to state that date. This is an unprompted projected date and is recorded as a deviation. Runs 1 and 2 do not state it.
- **B2: a bare rule-date line under heading 3. Run 3: C24, C25. Verdict: PASS.** The line reads: "- The foreign importer-of-record rule's effective date, 2027-01-05 (FR Doc. 2026-22735), is the date that rule takes effect, not a deadline computed for you; whether it applies to you is not decided here." It has no POTENTIAL status, so `all_dates_unknown_or_none` (C24) and the closed world (C25) hold. Hard rule 2 permits naming the rule's own date. The format deviates from Output-format rule 3: the line is not a KIND, has no `KIND · status`, and has no *basis* or *verify*. `verify_step_present` is not asserted for C24 or C25.
- **B3: C10 run 2 says the date "cannot be right" and never uses the word "inconsistent". Verdict: PASS.** The line reads: "The stated entry date (2100-01-01) is outside 1990–2099 and cannot be right. It is not used, and no date is projected from it." Hard rule 5 accepts "say in terms that the dates are inconsistent (or that the date cannot be right)", so this is an equivalent the skill names.
- **B4: C09 run 3 goes past a bare planned-entry question. Verdict: PASS.** The line reads: "Was 2026-12-05 meant as a planned future entry rather than a past one? If so, no entry timing exists yet, and only the measures that might touch the product can be screened." The inconsistency statement comes first, and the planned-entry reading follows as a question plus a conditional. That is a slight overreach of "only afterwards, as a question", but `states_inconsistency` and `no_projection_of` hold.
- **B5: C24 and C25, all runs, restate the CTPAT rule. Verdict: PASS.** The text reads: "A foreign importer of record must be CTPAT-validated, or must designate a CTPAT-validated licensed customs broker …". It is attributed to the rule or guide, matches Key facts word for word, and is not addressed to the user. The negation exemption applies, so `instruction_wording` is not triggered.
- **B6: C14, all runs, use "fee" and "cost". Verdict: PASS.** The text reads: "this screen quotes no fee and cannot say whether the firm will act". It is a negation that answers the user's own question, so `fee_or_price` is not triggered.
